One Rummy Deal, Turn by Turn

What happens in a single deal of 13-card rummy

1. Deal

WHAT YOU DO

You receive 13 cards; a wild joker is revealed

TIP

Note which rank is wild before sorting

2. Sort

WHAT YOU DO

Tap "sort" and group cards by suit

TIP

Look for runs of two or three cards in a suit

3. Pick

WHAT YOU DO

Take the top open card or a closed card

TIP

Pick from the open pile only if it completes a group

4. Discard

WHAT YOU DO

Throw away one card to end your turn

TIP

Discard high, unconnected cards first

5. Repeat

WHAT YOU DO

Keep picking and discarding as hands improve

TIP

Watch what others pick from the open pile

6. Declare

WHAT YOU DO

Place your 14th card on "finish" and show groups

TIP

Check you have a pure sequence and a second sequence

If your opening hand has no pairs, no runs and no jokers, dropping before your first pick is usually the cheapest option. Our rummy rules guide explains scoring and drops in detail.
